YOUR FIRST STEPS

A little preparation. A whole world to explore.

01 / INSTALL

Find your home.

Save PNLauncher.exe inside your PN-Client folder. For a fresh install, choose an empty, writable folder.

02 / PREPARE

Make it yours.

Connect to the PN LAN and run the launcher. Check for updates, then open Settings → Game settings to choose your display and sound.

03 / EXPLORE

Return to Midgard.

Choose Play Ragnarok. The launcher verifies the release and starts your adventure. Use Verify & repair if a client file needs fixing.

✧

A COMFORTABLE ADVENTURE

Your game, your setup.

OpenSetup adds display, renderer, sound and effects controls. Close Ragnarok before applying changes, and use the launcher’s settings backup to keep a copy of your preferences.
