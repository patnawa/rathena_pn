# PN Adventure services

Start with `@adventure`, or click My Adventure in the center of `@office`.
The Office is now one compact hall, with five NPC groups and a searchable
Directory. Update your client before visiting the new hall.

The [October 7 player experience update](player_experience_20261007.md) adds
`@goal` for a pinned live equipment plan, `@prepare` for combined preparation,
`@partyboard` for join requests and leader approval, and `@challenge` for the
rotating Weekly Expedition pilot. These features require the matching server
release; source implementation alone does not make them available in production.

| Feature | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Session dashboard | Choose a short activity, next story objective, equipment goal, or instance/cooldown check. Existing entrance requirements still apply. |
| Preparation presets | In town, save Farming, Boss or Support equipment and loot filters. Carry the exact saved gear to apply it. Edit supply targets, preview missing amounts and the total retail price, then confirm a restock. |
| Inventory tools | Preview a batch of ordinary items for main-storage deposit. Search your accessible personal pages. Set an explicit junk allowlist and preview a sale before confirming. Favorite, equipped, refined, carded, optioned, bound, unique, graded and rental items are protected. |
| Party recruitment | A party leader posts an activity, wanted role, beginner preference and note for 30 minutes. Browse and whisper the leader for an invitation. Readiness checks remain available. |
| Weekly adventures | Opt in and complete any three of seven objectives. Hunt, lab measurement and build review still work; rotating region hunts, three-city exploration and eligible solo/party boss clears add alternatives. Monday 00:00 UTC / 07:00 Bangkok reset. Stamps and recognition are cosmetic. |
| Guaranteed boss materials | Collect an eligible Alice or Bioresearch Battle boss clear reward to earn one credit for that character's corresponding track per daily reset. Choose a listed ingredient in the material exchange. Credits and delivery use the durable pointshop receipt. |
| Build sharing and lab challenges | Save a Build Notebook, then explicitly publish its note/stats and current equipment snapshot. Give friends the share code; replace or unpublish it whenever you want. Public shares expire after 30 days. Fixed 60-second lab targets, a personal DPS goal and matching-target/buff/build personal bests support repeatable practice. |

Preparation never changes stats or skills. Missing or altered equipment refuses
the switch. Switching respects native equipment restrictions and status effects.
Retail supplies are potions and wings; charges use current database prices.
Presets and goals belong to the character. Storage searches show owned,
accessible pages; they do not withdraw anything.

Boss credit tracks reset at 04:00 UTC / 11:00 Bangkok. Alice Normal and Hard
share one Alice daily credit. Bioresearch Story mode does not contain the
qualifying boss. Original instance rewards and random drops remain intact.
Summoned mobs, lab dummies, repeat collection and ineligible party members
cannot produce boss credits. See [Weekly adventures and reward rules](weekly_rewards_20261005.md)
for the actual ingredients and costs.

Recruitment does not reserve or enter instances. Listings disappear when the
leader disconnects, loses leadership, or the timer expires. Public build
snapshots are advice and cannot apply another character's gear or skills.
The lab challenges measure damage rotations on inert targets; they do not
simulate combat survival. Personal bests exclude incomplete or changing-buff
runs and keep different targets or server builds separate.

Registry-backed notes, presets, cosmetic progress and public snapshots follow
normal server save behavior. Paid inventory and boss-material purchases use
durable receipts. After a delayed purchase, check Purchase History before
retrying. Never assume that closing a shop canceled an already submitted
transaction.
